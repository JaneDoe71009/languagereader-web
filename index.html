Before your first lookup

A small note on privacy.

Books are kept in this browser and, when signed in, on your account server. Looking up a word sends that word to Wiktionary and, when needed, MyMemory for a free English translation.

If sentence translation is on, its surrounding sentence is also sent to MyMemory. These services receive your IP address and process requests under their own policies. Translation can be imperfect and daily limits may apply.

For quick hover meanings, visible words are prepared in the background through Wiktionary and, when needed, MyMemory. A new word can take a moment to prepare. When gradual translation is on, selected words in the reading area are sent to MyMemory for translation into your chosen language. Results are cached in this browser. Turning ordinary sentence lookups off does not turn gradual translation off.
